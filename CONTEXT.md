# Noema

Noema is a personal AI workspace: the place a person thinks, writes, and gets things done, which gets smarter the more they use it.

## Language

**Marketing page**:
The public page where someone who does not yet have an account meets Noema.
_Avoid_: Landing page, Home, splash

**Home**:
The in-app place that opens onto the whole workspace.
_Avoid_: Marketing page, dashboard

**Prospective tester**:
A person who already keeps notes and uses a chatbot, does not yet know Noema, and would be on the invite list.
_Avoid_: Customer, visitor, user
